# Newsletter Sponsorship Outreach Templates

Use these templates to pitch small finance newsletters (1K-10K subscribers) for $25-$50 sponsored mentions.

**Where to find newsletters:**
- [Swapstack.co](https://swapstack.co) — filter by "Finance", sort by price ascending
- [Paved.com](https://paved.com) — newsletter ad marketplace
- [Substack search](https://substack.com) — search "daily market brief", "stock market newsletter"
- Direct outreach to FinTwit accounts with newsletters

**Budget:** $100 total (2 placements at $50 each, or 3-4 at $25-$35)
